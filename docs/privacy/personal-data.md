# Personal data in Bugaboo

GENERATED by `cargo xtask gen privacy` from the personal-data marks in the code (ADR-0015). Do not edit this page by hand.

Every stored field says whether it holds personal data. This page lists the answers. The same marks drive *Download my data*, *Remove a person*, the keep-and-delete rules and the record of processing (GDPR Art. 30).

**121 entities, 670 fields: 202 personal, 468 not personal.**

## Categories

| Category        | What it covers                                                                                                                                                       |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `identity`      | Who a person is: name, e-mail address, avatar.                                                                                                                       |
| `credentials`   | What proves who a person is: password hash, passkeys, backup codes.                                                                                                  |
| `activity`      | What a person did and when: who changed what, who is assigned.                                                                                                       |
| `organization`  | Where a person works: their groups and the roles they have, and where (ADR-0025).                                                                                    |
| `content`       | What a person wrote: comments, descriptions, the reasons of other changes.                                                                                           |
| `employee_time` | Time at work: work days and breaks, time entries with their notes and accounting fields, the running timer, and the reasons for changing a past work day (ADR-0035). |
| `technical`     | What identifies a device or connection: network address, browser, session.                                                                                           |

## Legal bases

| Legal basis            | What it means                                                                                 |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `consent`              | The person agreed (Art. 6(1)(a)).                                                             |
| `contract`             | Needed for a contract with the person (Art. 6(1)(b)).                                         |
| `legal_obligation`     | A law requires it (Art. 6(1)(c)).                                                             |
| `vital_interests`      | Needed to protect someone's life (Art. 6(1)(d)).                                              |
| `public_task`          | Needed for a task in the public interest (Art. 6(1)(e)).                                      |
| `legitimate_interests` | The organization has a good reason that does not override the person's rights (Art. 6(1)(f)). |

## Limits of the marks

- A field is marked `#[not_personal]` only if its type is on a short list (numbers, truth values, moments, IDs of records, the words on this page, and types that say so). Text and containers of unknown content are not on it, so they are marked as personal data.
- The ID of a person is personal data: a pseudonym that is fully random. After a person is removed it is kept only as the link to the *Former user …* tombstone.
- A general record ID (`EntityId`) can point to a person's record. It counts as not personal here, and the person is covered by the marks on the actor and the changes of the event.
- The marks say whether a field holds personal data. Whether its category and legal basis are the right ones is decided in review.
