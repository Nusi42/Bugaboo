WP-01 uses F01 and up, WP-02 F10 and up, WP-03 F20 and up, WP-04 F30 and up, WP-05 F40 and up, WP-06 F50 and up, WP-07 F60 and up, WP-08 F70 and up, WP-09 F80 and up, WP-10 F90 and up, WP-11 F100 and up.
