# ADR-0013: AI: one provider interface, one tool registry, no decisions about people

Status: **Accepted** · 2026-10-04

## Context

AI is priority three, but must never bypass permissions, must resist prompt injection, and must stay outside the AI Act's high-risk and banned uses.

## Decision

- **One provider trait** with adapters for Anthropic (default) and OpenAI-compatible endpoints (covers local models).
- **One tool registry** used by both the assistant and MCP. Each tool calls a `bb-app` use case as the actor.
- **Assistant writes are proposals**; nothing changes until the person clicks *Apply*.
- **Context builder** sends only readable, non-excluded fields and fences people's text as untrusted.
- **Designed out:** tools that rank, evaluate or assign people, read working hours, or analyse mood. They don't exist in the registry.
- AI output saved into issues or documents is marked *Written with the assistant*.

## Consequences

- MCP and the assistant can't drift apart in rules or capabilities.
- Adding a tool needs a review against the AI Act rules (checklist in the ADR template).

## Alternatives considered

- **Separate tool code for MCP and the assistant:** duplicates permission handling. Rejected.
- **Letting the assistant act directly:** prompt-injection risk. Rejected.

## Amendment (M7, where the parts live, the settings and what goes to a provider)

The maintainer accepted every recommendation of M7-Q1 to Q10 on 2026-11-07. What changes here:

- **Where the parts live** (architecture §4). Every tool is a `bb-app` use case run as the actor, the assistant is a `bb-app` use case, and `bb-app` imports no adapter (architecture §4, rule 2). So **the tool registry and the context builder live in `bb-app`** (`crates/bb-app/src/ai/`), next to the use cases they call, and do no I/O of their own. **`bb-ai` holds the provider adapters** (Anthropic and OpenAI-compatible) behind the port `AiProvider` of `bb-app`, and the test fake behind its feature `testing`; `bb-server` wires the adapter. **`bb-mcp` turns the registry into MCP** with `rmcp` and depends on `bb-app` only ([ADR-0042](0042-mcp-server-and-how-ai-apps-sign-in.md)). Enforced by: ARCH-DEPS, ARCH-AI-TOOLS (it reads `crates/bb-app/src/ai/tools/**` and `crates/bb-mcp/**`).
- **The settings of AI** (topic *AI* of the registry, ADR-0018 amendment M7; *Organization settings › Connections › AI*, UX §5.1):
  - AI is **off for the organization** at the start (AI-02, PRIV-08), and each project has a switch that follows the project above until it has its own; AI counts at a place only when the organization and that place have it on (one function, *AI counts here*, which the assistant, MCP and agents ask);
  - the **AI provider** (*Anthropic* or *OpenAI-compatible*, which covers a *Local model*), the **model**, the **address** (for OpenAI-compatible) and the **API key**, kept as a stored secret under the master key (architecture §7, *Secrets and keys*) and never shown again; a change of the provider or the address asks for the administrator's password again, only HTTPS goes to an outside provider, an address on the local network is allowed only for the OpenAI-compatible choice, and *Try the provider* is the one call that may be made while AI is off: it sends only the fixed try text and no data of the organization, needs the agreement of an outside provider first, and is limited to one try in 10 seconds per person; an error of the provider becomes one sentence (*"The AI provider did not answer"*) and is never shown in its own words (it can repeat what the internal network answered);
  - **the agreement step:** before an outside provider is switched on, the administrator confirms that a *Data processing agreement* is in place (PRIV-08); a local model needs none. The confirmation is stored with who gave it, when and for which provider, and is in the security log; changing the provider asks again;
  - ***What goes where***: the page lists what may be sent (the text and the fields the person reads, without *Never sent to AI*), to which provider and in which country;
  - ***Send names of people*** (M7-Q5, off at the start), and whether AI apps may sign in, with the web return addresses an administrator allows (ADR-0042, decision 3).
  Enforced by: ARCH-SETTINGS, and the tests `ai_02_ai_is_off_at_the_start_and_no_call_leaves_the_server` and `priv_08_an_outside_provider_needs_the_agreement_before_it_is_switched_on` (WP-02).
- ***Never sent to AI* in M7** (AI-03) is a mark on a **field** and on a **project** (with its sub-projects): the context builder leaves the field or the project's issues out even for a person who reads them, and a search sent through a tool that names a field kept from AI is refused like an unknown field (QRY-05). Administrators mark fields and any project (*Change organization settings*); a project lead marks their own project (*Change project settings* there). Restricted issue groups follow when restricted issues come (SEC-05, 1.0; M7-Q10). It holds for every way AI reaches Bugaboo, because all of them read through the one registry and the one context builder: the assistant, AI apps through MCP and agents. Enforced by: the tests `ai_03_a_field_never_sent_to_ai_is_not_in_the_context_even_when_the_person_reads_it`, `ai_03_a_project_never_sent_to_ai_keeps_its_sub_projects_out_too` and `ai_03_a_search_through_a_tool_that_names_a_field_kept_from_ai_is_refused_like_an_unknown_field`, and TEST-PROMPT-INJECTION.
- **Names of people (M7-Q5).** With an outside provider, a person in the context is sent as their role label (*"a Team member"*), and the asking person as *"you"*, never by name, handle or e-mail address, unless an administrator switched on *Send names of people*; *What goes where* says which is in force. A local model gets people as the asking person sees them (*Who sees people*). MCP is not affected: an AI app acts as the person and reads people as the person reads them (AI-04). Enforced by: the test `ai_04_for_an_outside_provider_people_are_named_by_role_and_the_asker_as_you`, and SEC-MATRIX.
- **The conversation of the assistant (M7-Q6)** lives in the open panel only and is not stored on the server; closing the panel ends it.
- **The log of a tool call (M7-Q6)** is one row with who (the person, the actor kind, the app or the agent), which tool, *reads* or *changes*, the size in and out, the time and the outcome; never the content of either. It is kept 90 days, then deleted. A refused call also writes a row of the security log. Content logging for troubleshooting is not built in M7 (architecture §7, *AI*, changes with this). Enforced by: DATA-LOGS.
- **The author marks of untrusted text.** The context builder and every tool answer fence each text of a person (a title, a description, a comment, a field value, a file name) as data, with an **author mark** that says who wrote it: *someone in the project*, *a Guest*, *someone outside*, *an agent*, *the assistant* (AI-05, ADR-0029 decision 9). A fence cannot be closed from inside the text. Enforced by: TEST-PROMPT-INJECTION.
- **What the assistant does in M7 (M7-Q2).** The panel answers what the person types about what it looks at (the issue, the list, the project), with the read tools, and proposes changes with the write tools; *"Summarize the comments on WEB-142"* works as a typed question. The buttons of AI-12, AI-13 and AI-14, the catch-up and the suggestions beside the fields stay in M12.
- **Usage limits and cost budgets** per person and organization (AI-06) stay in M12 (M7-Q10); M7 has only the limits of agents and keys (ADR-0029, amendment M7).

## Enforced by
