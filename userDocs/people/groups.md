# Groups

A group is a set of people. Give a role to a group, and everybody in it has that role. Groups hold people only, never other groups.

## Creating and changing a group

*Organization settings › Groups* lists the groups. Type a name under "Create a group" and press "Create the group": a new group is empty. Press a group's name in the list to open it next to the list (on a phone it takes the whole page).

In the open group you can:

- **Rename** it.
- See what it is **used for**: "Used for the whole organization and in 4 projects you can see". The number counts only the projects you can see.
- See **the people in it**, and take one out with "Take out".
- **Put people in**: search by name, and press "Put in" next to the person. The people you picked last come first when the search box is empty. People who are in already say so.
- **Delete the group**.

Taking a person out, and putting a person in, happen at once and say so with **Undo** for 10 seconds. If taking yourself out of a group, or deleting a group you are in, would take away your own way to manage roles, Bugaboo asks first. A person who is put into a group gets every role of the group, so Bugaboo checks that you hold all of those roles yourself, as for giving the roles directly: if you do not, you are told so.

## Deleting a group

The dialog says exactly what happens: the group is deleted, and every role and every No access it was given goes with it. The people in it stay, and keep the roles they have in other ways. It cannot be taken back with Undo.

If the group has a No access on a project, taking people out of the group, or deleting it, lifts that No access for them. That is why you need the permission "Decide who has which role" in each such project too: a person who may only manage groups cannot lift a No access this way.
