# People

*Organization settings › People* lists everybody who has an account, by name.

## The list

Each row shows the name and the e-mail address, and, as far as you may see it, the groups the person is in and the roles they have. Type in the search box to find people by name or e-mail address. The list draws only the rows that are in view, so it stays quick when there are thousands of people. If there are more than fit on one page, "Show more people" asks for the next page.

Press a name to open the page of that person. It shows when the account was created, the groups, and the roles they have in the whole organization and in the projects where you decide who has which role. A role in a project you may not know about is not listed at all.

Each role opens the list where it is changed: *Roles and permissions* for the whole organization, *Who has which role* in the settings of the project for a project. You cannot change a person's roles on their page: change them where the rows are, in [Groups](groups.md) and in [Who has which role](who-has-which-role.md).

## Correct a name or an e-mail address

On the page of a person, **Correct name** and **Correct e-mail address** change what is wrong, for example after a marriage or a typo. Each always asks for a reason, in a few words. The change counts at once: the person signs in with the corrected address from then on. The person is told in the app and by e-mail with your reason, and for an address both the old and the new address get an e-mail. The security log records who corrected what of whom; of the old value it keeps only a fingerprint.

An address that another account has is refused. People correct their own name and address in *My settings › Profile* (see [Profile](../my-settings/profile.md)).

## Invitations

An invitation is a link that lets one person create their account. It works once and runs out after 7 days. Type the person's e-mail address under "Invitations", press "Create the invitation" and send them the link yourself: Bugaboo does not send invitations by e-mail. The link is shown only once.

The invitations that nobody has used yet are listed below, each with "Take back".

## Signing up without an invitation

People with the permission "Change organization settings" also see the rule for who may create their own account without an invitation, on the same page.

Signing up needs e-mail. A person gives only their e-mail address, and Bugaboo sends a link to it that works once and for a limited time, which is on [Times and counts](../notifications/numbers.md). Opening it lets the person choose a name and a password, and the account is created then. While e-mail is off (see [the mail server](../notifications/mail-server.md)), signing up is closed, and the switch says so.
