# People

*Organization settings › People* lists everybody who has an account, by name.

## The list

Each row shows the name and the e-mail address, and, as far as you may see it, the groups the person is in and the roles they have. Type in the search box to find people by name or e-mail address. The list draws only the rows that are in view, so it stays quick when there are thousands of people. If there are more than fit on one page, "Show more people" asks for the next page.

Press a name to open the page of that person. It shows when the account was made, the groups, and the roles they have in the whole organization and in the projects where you decide who has which role. A role in a project you may not know about is not listed at all.

You cannot change a person's roles on their page: change them where the rows are, in [Groups](groups.md) and in [Who has which role](who-has-which-role.md).

## Invitations

An invitation is a link that lets one person make their account. It works once and runs out after 7 days. Type the person's e-mail address under "Invitations", press "Make an invitation" and send them the link yourself: Bugaboo cannot send e-mail yet. The link is shown only once.

The invitations that nobody has used yet are listed below, each with "Take back".

## Signing up without an invitation

People with the permission "Change organization settings" also see the rule for who may make their own account without an invitation, on the same page.
