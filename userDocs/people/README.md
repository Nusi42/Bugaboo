# People, groups and roles

This part is for the people who look after who may do what: administrators, and project leads for their own projects. It is in *Organization settings* under "People" in the menu, and in the *Project settings* of each project.

- [People](people.md): everybody who has an account, and the invitations
- [Groups](groups.md): sets of people, to give a role to many people at once
- [Roles and permissions](roles-and-permissions.md): what a role allows, creating your own roles, and what happens when you delete one
- [Who has which role](who-has-which-role.md): giving a role in the whole organization or in a project, rows that come from a project above, and No access

## Who sees what here

Each page is there only for the people who hold its permission, and a page you may not open is "Not found", as if it did not exist:

| Page | You need |
|--------|----------|
| People (the list of everybody, with e-mail addresses) | "See everybody's e-mail address and correct their names" |
| People › Invitations | "Invite people and take invitations back" |
| People › Signing up without an invitation | "Change organization settings" |
| Groups | "Create, rename and delete groups and change who is in them" |
| Roles and permissions (the roles) | "Create, change and delete roles" |
| Who has which role (whole organization, and in a project) | "Decide who has which role", where the rows are |

A project lead has the last permission for their own projects only. They see **Who has which role** in the *Project settings* of those projects, and nothing of the organization's other pages or projects.

People who work on these pages see other people's names, because they need them. They never see numbers that include things they may not see: "Used in 4 projects" counts only the projects you can see.

## Nobody gives what they do not have

You can give a role, or add a permission to a role, only when you hold every permission of it yourself in that place. And Bugaboo never leaves the organization without somebody who can manage roles: a change that would do that is refused, with a sentence that says why.
