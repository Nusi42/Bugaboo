# Choices, colors and looks

A *Pick one* or *Pick several* field offers a list of **choices**. The page of the field has a section for them.

## Choices

- **Add** a choice at the end with its name. Names of the choices of one field must differ, whatever the capital letters.
- **Reorder** with the arrow buttons next to each choice, which move it up or down.
- **Color and icon.** Each choice has one of twelve colors and, if you like, an icon. The twelve colors belong to the theme, so every color can be read in all four themes. A color never carries the meaning alone: the name of the choice is always written next to it.
- **Retire** a choice to stop offering it for new values. Issues that have it keep it. Offer it again with the same switch.
- **Combine with another choice** moves every value of one choice to another one, and retires the first. An issue that had both keeps one. The history of every issue it touches says what changed, and the issues get a new version. This cannot be taken back, so Bugaboo asks you to choose the other choice first.

## Look

The **Look** of a field says how its values are shown:

- **Badge**: a small colored label with the icon of the choice
- **Pill**: a rounded colored label
- **Plain text**
- **Progress bar**: for numbers, between the lowest and the highest value of the rules (or out of 100)
- **Avatar**: for people

A field offers only the looks that fit its kind. The page shows the look in all four themes side by side, so you can see before you save that every color is readable.
