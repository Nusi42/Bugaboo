# Notification settings

*Notification settings* are in *My settings*, and *Customize* at the top right of the page of notifications opens them. It has one row for each kind of event, and two switches in each row:

- **In the app** puts the event under *Notifications*.
- **By e-mail** sends you an e-mail as well. E-mail goes only when your organization has switched it on (see [the mail server](mail-server.md)).

A choice shows at once and is saved in the background. **Reset to default** takes your own choices away, so the standard ones of Bugaboo and your organization count again.

An administrator can lock a choice for everyone. You then see it with **Set by your organization**, and cannot change it.

## What an e-mail says

An e-mail is plain text. It says the issue ID and title, who changed what, and the comment, only with what you may see, and gives the address of the issue in Bugaboo. The text of a comment is quoted line by line. Changes to one issue within 5 minutes come in one e-mail.

An organization can choose **Short e-mails**. They leave out all issue text (titles and comments) and only say that something happened, with the address to open.
