# Notifications

*Notifications* is in the menu, with the number of notifications you have not read yet. On a phone it is in the bar at the bottom. The keys **g** then **n** open it from anywhere.

## What you are told about

Standard choices, which you change under [Notification settings](customize.md):

| What happens | In the app | By e-mail |
|---|---|---|
| You are mentioned | yes | yes |
| An issue is assigned to you | yes | yes |
| A status change, a comment or a file on an issue you follow | yes | no |
| Other changes to fields of an issue you follow | no | no |
| Your change to many issues has ended | yes | no |
| Somebody asks you to finish editing, or takes your editing over | yes | no |

You never hear of your own changes. You hear of an issue only while you may see it, and you see in a notification only the fields you may see. People are shown the way *Who sees people* says: by name, by their role, or as *Someone*.

## The page

The notifications are grouped by issue. The issue with the newest notification comes first, and the newest notification is first in its group. An unread one has the word **New** and a colored bar at its side.

- **Open** goes to the issue and to the exact comment or change. The comment or change is marked.
- **Mark as read** takes one notification off the unread ones. **Mark all as read** does it for all of them.
- **All**, **Unread** and **Mentions** at the top choose what the list shows.
- **Customize**, at the top right, opens [Notification settings](customize.md).

A notification that tells you a change to many issues has ended has **Show the result**.

The page shows the newest first and **Show earlier** adds older ones. Notifications are deleted after 90 days.

## The start page

The start page shows your newest unread notifications. **Show all notifications** goes to the page.

## Keeping up

Bugaboo asks for the number on the menu every half minute and when you come back to its tab.
