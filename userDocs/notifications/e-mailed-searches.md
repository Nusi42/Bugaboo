# E-mailed searches

**E-mail me this search** sends you a saved search by e-mail, on the weekdays and at the hour you choose. It goes to you only: to the e-mail address of your account, never to anybody else.

## Getting a saved search by e-mail

1. Open a saved search: one of yours, or one somebody shared with you.
2. Choose **E-mail me this search** beside it.
3. Tick the days, choose the hour, and choose **E-mail me this search**.

The hour is in your own time zone (*My settings › Region*). When the clocks change for summer time, the e-mail still comes at the hour you chose.

**Only when something is new or changed** is on at the start: then no e-mail comes when none of the issues the search finds changed since the last e-mail. Bugaboo keeps only when the last e-mail went, never which issues it held.

The e-mails of one hour go out over that hour, so yours may come a few minutes after the full hour.

## What the e-mail holds

The e-mail is plain text: no pictures, no attachments. It holds:

- a link to the search in Bugaboo and a link to your e-mailed searches, first;
- the name of the search;
- up to 50 issues in the order of the search, each with its ID, its status, a link and its title;
- and how many more issues the search finds, when there are more.

The search runs when the e-mail is sent, with your rights at that moment: you get only the issues and the fields you may see in Bugaboo then. The links open Bugaboo and ask you to sign in; nothing in the e-mail changes anything.

The e-mail leaves Bugaboo and reaches your mailbox through your organization's mail server. If your organization uses **Short e-mails**, the e-mail has no titles and no name of the search: only IDs, statuses and links.

## Your e-mailed searches

Your e-mailed searches are listed at the end of *My settings › Notification settings*, each with its days and hour. **Change** opens the same window. **Stop** takes it off the list; **Undo** brings it back. You can have up to 20.

## When nothing is sent

- **E-mail or the feature is switched off.** E-mail goes only when your organization has switched it on (see [the mail server](mail-server.md)), and an administrator can switch **E-mail me this search** off for everyone. While either is off nothing is sent, and the e-mails come again by themselves when both are on.
- **You can no longer open the search.** When the search was deleted, is no longer shared with you, or no longer works with what you may see, your list says *Sends nothing: you can no longer open this search*, and nothing is sent until you set it again.
- **You were switched off.** Nothing is sent to a person who is switched off. When you are switched on again, your e-mailed searches wait until you change them.

When you are removed from Bugaboo, your e-mailed searches are deleted with your account, and an e-mailed search is deleted with its saved search. *Download my data* holds your e-mailed searches: the search, the days and the hour.
