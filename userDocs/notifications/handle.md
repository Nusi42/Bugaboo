# Your handle

Your handle is the short name people type after `@` to mention you, like `@ana.brandt`. Bugaboo creates it from your name when your account is created. You see and change it under *My settings › Handle*.

## The rules

- A handle has only the letters a to z (no accents: `ä` is written `ae`), digits, dots, dashes and underscores, and at most 64 characters. A name with none of them gets the handle `person`.
- Nobody else may have it, and it cannot be the handle of a group or a role.
- You can change it once in 7 days. The handle you were given with your account does not count as a change.
- A handle you give up stays reserved for you. Nobody else can take it. Comments written before still name you, because a comment keeps the person and not the handle. A new comment that uses the old handle names nobody.

If a handle is refused, the page says why: it is not a handle, somebody has or had it (or a group or a role has it), or you changed yours a short time ago.

Your handle follows *Who sees people*: people who may not see names do not see it.
