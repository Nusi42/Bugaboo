# Signing in

## With a password

Open Bugaboo, type your e-mail address and your password and press "Sign in". If your account has a second check, Bugaboo asks for it next.

If something is wrong, Bugaboo says so in plain words. After many wrong tries in a short time you have to wait a little before you try again. The wait grows with every failed try.

## With a passkey

A passkey lets you sign in with the fingerprint, face or screen lock of your device, instead of typing a password. Press "Sign in with a passkey" on the sign-in page.

To add a passkey, sign in and open *My settings* and then "Sign-in & security". Give the passkey a name that tells you which device it is on, type your password again, and press the button to add it. A passkey belongs to the web address of Bugaboo. If the address changes, the passkey no longer works. Sign in with your password and add it again.

## The second step

If your account asks for a second check, Bugaboo shows "One more step" after your password:

- Type the six-digit **code** from your code app. Each code works once.
- If you cannot use the app, type one of your **backup codes**. Each backup code works once. In "Sign-in & security" you can make new backup codes, and the old ones stop working.
- If you have a passkey, press "Use a passkey".

To switch the code app on or off, open *My settings* and then "Sign-in & security". Bugaboo asks for your password again before it changes anything.

## Where you're signed in

"Where you're signed in" lists every place where you are signed in, for example each browser, with the browser, the system and when it was last used. The place you are in now is marked "This browser". If you see a place you do not know, press the button next to it to sign out of it.

"Sign out everywhere" ends all your places at once, this one included. Use it when you lost a device. Afterwards, change your password.

## Invitations and making your own account

An administrator invites people on the page "Invitations". Bugaboo cannot send e-mail yet, so the administrator copies the link and sends it to the person. The link works once and runs out after 7 days. The person opens it, chooses a name and a password, and the account is made. An administrator can take back an invitation that is still open.

An administrator can also let people make their own account, from chosen e-mail domains. The page "Invitations" shows this setting. If you remove all domains, anybody who can reach Bugaboo can make an account. Bugaboo warns you and asks you to confirm.

If making your own account is switched off, the page "Make an account" says so and asks you to ask an administrator for an invitation.
