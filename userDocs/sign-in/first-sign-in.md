# Your first sign-in

The first time Bugaboo runs, nobody has an account. You create the first one, and it becomes the administrator. An administrator can invite people and look after the server.

You need the setup code. [Where to find it](../install/README.md#the-setup-code).

1. Open the address of Bugaboo in your browser. Bugaboo shows "Welcome to Bugaboo" by itself.
2. **Setup code.** Type the code. Capital letters and hyphens do not matter. A wrong code sends you back to this step.
3. **Your account.** Type your name, your e-mail address and a password. Bugaboo explains next to the field when a password is too short or too common. A few words together give a good password.
4. **Keep it safe.** A password alone can be guessed or stolen. You can add a second check now, or later in "Sign-in & security":
   - A **passkey** lets you sign in with the fingerprint, face or screen lock of your device.
   - A **code app** on your phone shows a new six-digit code every 30 seconds. Scan the picture with the app and type the code it shows. Bugaboo then shows your **backup codes**. They are shown only once. Save them somewhere safe, then press "I have saved them".
5. **Done.** Press "Open Bugaboo". You are signed in.

The setup closes for good after the first account. The address `/setup` then leads to the sign-in page.

## After the setup

- Invite people: [Invitations and creating your own account](sign-in.md#invitations-and-creating-your-own-account).
- Check that the server is well: the administrator's page "Server status" shows the version, how long Bugaboo has been running, the database, when the HTTPS certificate runs out, and whether the security log is intact.
