# Building a dashboard

Open your dashboard and press **Change** (on *Home*, **Customize**). The line says **Changing**, and each box gets a handle on its title, a menu, and a corner to resize it. **Done** ends changing. Each change is saved at once and offers **Undo**.

A new dashboard opens in **Change** by itself.

## Add box

**Add box** opens a window with the ready-made boxes, each with a preview of what it shows you now:

- **My open issues**: issues assigned to you that are not done, the most urgent first
- **Due soon**: issues due this week that are not done
- **Open bugs by priority**: bars of the bugs that are not done, by priority
- **Issues by status group**: bars of every issue by *Not started*, *In progress* and *Done*
- **Progress of a release**: bars by status group of the issues planned for a release
- **My time this week**: your own time, one bar for each day
- **Time on this search**: *Estimated time*, *Time left* and *Time spent* of the issues that are not done
- **Small timeline of a search**: the issues that are not done, with their dates
- **My timer**: your timer and work day
- **Recent changes in this project**: the newest changes

Press **Add** under one to put it at the bottom of the dashboard. Under the ready-made boxes, **A box of your own** lets you choose the kind, the title and the search, in the search language of the search bar. For **Numbers and chart**, **Group by** chooses the field of the bars, or **Nothing: one number**. For **Time totals**, **Whose time** chooses the issues of the search or your own week.

A dashboard holds at most 20 boxes. If a dashboard you created holds boxes you cannot see now (their search names something you may no longer read), they count too, and Bugaboo says so. A box whose search has a mistake is not added, and Bugaboo says why.

## Place and resize a box

- Drag a box by its **title** to put it somewhere else. While you drag, the other boxes stay where they are and a shadow shows where the box will land; the others move aside only when you let go.
- Drag the **corner** at the bottom right to change the width or the height of the box.

The boxes always move up as far as they can: there is never an empty row above a box. A box moved below others in its column moves back up if nothing stands above it.

### With the keyboard

Open the menu of a box (the button **Actions for** and its title), or press the handle before the title of the box (**Place** and its title), which starts **Move…**:

- **Move…**: the arrow keys move the box, left and right by a column, up before the box above it and down after the box below it.
- **Resize…**: the arrow keys change it by a column narrower or wider, a row lower or higher.

The shadow shows where the box will be, and screen readers say its column and row. **Enter** keeps it, **Escape** goes back, and so does leaving the box with **Tab** or ending with **Done**. While you drag a box with the pointer, **Escape** lets go without moving it, and near the bottom of the window the page scrolls.

### On a phone

A phone shows the boxes in one column, in the order of the grid: the top row from left to right, then the next row. In **Change**, the menu of a box has **Move up** and **Move down** instead of a drag.

## Change or remove a box

**Change box** in the menu of a box changes its title, its search and the options of its kind. **Remove** takes it off the dashboard and offers **Undo**.
