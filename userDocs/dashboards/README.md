# Dashboards

A dashboard is a page of boxes. Each box reads a search and shows it: the first issues it finds, a chart of how many there are in each group, the time on them, a small timeline, your timer, or the newest changes. You create a dashboard for yourself and can share it with a group or a project.

- [Building a dashboard](building-a-dashboard.md): *Add box*, the ready-made boxes, placing and resizing boxes, by pointer and by keyboard

## Where your dashboards are

**Dashboards** in the sidebar lists the dashboards you created under **Mine** and the ones others shared with you under **Shared with me**, each with who created it. **Create a dashboard** at its end, and beside **Customize** on **Home**, asks for a name and opens the new dashboard. On a phone, **Dashboards** is in **More**.

Each dashboard has an address of its own, `/d/` and its ID. You can send it to someone you shared it with. Someone it is not shared with sees the page of an address that does not exist.

**Pin to sidebar** in the head of a dashboard puts it into **Pinned**, as for a project or a saved search.

## Each box shows what you may see

A box runs as the person who looks at it. Two people can see different numbers on the same dashboard, because each sees only the issues and the fields they are allowed to see.

A box you may not read at all is not shown to you, and the boxes under it move up, so nothing tells of it. A guest, who does not see due dates, does not see a box of issues due this week.

## The kinds of box

- **Search results**: the first ten issues of the search, in its order, with their status, Issue ID, priority and title. Press one to open it.
- **Numbers and chart**: how many issues the search finds, as one large number, or as bars for each group of a field (for example by priority). Each bar has its name and its number written beside it.
- **Time totals**: *Estimated time*, *Time left* and *Time spent* of the issues of a search, or your own time of this week, one bar for each day. Only your own time: never the time of others.
- **Small timeline**: the issues of a search with their dates and a short bar over seven weeks around today, as on a phone. Nothing is dragged here.
- **My timer**: your timer and your work day, as in the sidebar.
- **Recent changes**: the newest changes to the issues of a search, as the history of each issue writes them.

Every chart has **Show as table**, which shows the same numbers as a table; screen readers always read the table. A chart grouped by a person lists the people in the order of their names, never by who has the most. There are no pie charts.

When a sum adds up fewer issues than the box counts, because some issues have no value or you may not see it, it says so: "12h over 9 of 14 issues".

## Shared dashboards

A dashboard is private when you create it. **Share** shares it with groups and projects, as for a saved search; you need the permission *Share dashboards*. Only you change, share or delete your dashboard. Someone you shared it with sees who created it and can **Save a copy**, which is theirs and private and holds the boxes they see.

**More** › **Delete** moves your dashboard to the trash and offers **Undo**. **More** › **Rename** gives it a new name.

## Home

*Home*, your start page, is your own dashboard. Your notifications stay at the top; below them are your boxes, at first **My timer** and **Due soon** side by side and **My open issues** under them. On a phone your timer is the box **My timer**. **Customize** changes them like **Change** on a dashboard. *Home* is never shared.
