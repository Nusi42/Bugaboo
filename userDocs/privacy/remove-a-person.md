# Switch off and remove a person

A person's page (*Organization settings › People*, then the person) has the part **Sign-in and removal**.

## Switch off

**Switch off** (with the permission *Manage people*) ends the person's sign-in at once. A short question says what stops: their sessions end, their access keys and connected apps are taken back, their agents stop, and the invitations they sent are taken back. Every record stays, and so do the issues assigned to them, until somebody changes them. The page then says that the person is switched off.

**Switch on** gives the sign-in back. What was taken back stays taken back: the person creates new keys and connects their apps again.

## Remove a person

**Remove this person…** (with the permission *Remove people*, at the start: administrators) opens a window. Removing a person is final and cannot be undone, so the window says first what happens, from what Bugaboo stores about the person:

- **Deleted now**: the account and what only serves it, such as sign-ins, keys, settings, notifications, drafts and private saved searches, each with how many records.
- **Stays, shown as Former user**: the records of the organization, such as issues, comments and time entries. They still read, with *Former user K7Q2* where the name was.
- **Kept by law**: what the law keeps for its period, with the day until which it is kept. Work days keep the person's name for the people who may see working hours, until an administrator deletes them after their period on [Keep and delete rules](keep-rules-page.md).

Choose what happens to the comments the person wrote: **Keep their comments, shown as Former user** (what your organization chose at the start) or **Delete their comments**.

Only a switched-off person can be removed. If the person can still sign in, the window says so and offers **Switch off first**; the button **Remove** with the person's name waits until then.

After the removal the person's page shows *Former user* and the code of four signs in place of the name, the same label everywhere in Bugaboo. A file of *Download my data* about the person that was not fetched yet goes with the account: give it to the person first.
