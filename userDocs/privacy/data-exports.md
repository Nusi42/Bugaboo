# Data exports

*Organization settings › Privacy › Data exports* is for people with the permission *Download someone's data* (at the start: administrators). It lists every file of *Download my data*, the newest first, a page at a time:

- **Whose data**: the person the file is about.
- **Created by**: the person themselves, or the administrator who created it.
- **Why**: the reason an administrator gave; *Asked for by the person* for a person's own file.
- **Asked for**, **File** (*Being created*, *Ready* with its size, or *Could not be created*) and **Deleted on**: every file is deleted after the period that [Keep and delete rules](keep-and-delete.md) names for *Files of Download my data*, counted from when it was asked for.

The person who asked for a file downloads it with **Download** while it is ready.

## Download someone's data

When a person asks for their data in another way, for example by letter, an administrator creates the same file for them:

1. Choose **Download someone's data**. A window opens.
2. Under *Whose data*, search for the person and choose them. The list offers the people you may see by name, as *Who sees people* says for your role; the permission *Download someone's data* is enough, *Manage people* is not needed.
3. Under *Why*, give the reason in a few words, such as the person's request. A reason is always needed.
4. Type your password again, so that nobody who only sits at your browser can take the data of everybody.
5. Choose **Create the file**.

The file holds what the person's own *Download my data* holds, whatever else you may read. The person is told in the app and by e-mail, with your reason, also when they are switched off, and the security log records who created the file for whom and why. The new file stands at the top of the list.
