# Working hours of others

*Working hours of others* shows one other person's week, in the *Work day* rows only. You need the permission *See working hours of others*, which the role *HR* holds. Without it the page does not exist for you.

Pick one person by name, one at a time. No list puts people in an order by their hours, and time on issues is not shown here. The week is shown as it is until you choose *Change* (for which you need *Change working hours of others*). Every change needs a reason, and the person is told about it with your reason.

No list puts people in an order by their hours, and time on issues is not shown here.

While an administrator has switched on *Totals only*, you see each person's total for the week and nothing else, and nobody changes other people's days.
