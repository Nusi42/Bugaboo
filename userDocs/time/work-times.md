# Work times

Your work times say when and how much you usually work, and the days you are away. The timeline plans your issues with them: an issue gets as many of your work days as its estimate needs, and away days, days off and public holidays are skipped. Open them in **My settings › Work times**.

## Usual work days

The seven days of the week stand in the order your *Region* starts the week. Each has a tick, for a day you usually work, and its **work hours** (at the start Monday to Friday, 8 hours each). The line above sums it up, for example "Mon–Thu, 6h a day". A tick and the hours save as soon as you change them, and **Undo** takes the change back for a few seconds. The hours of a day without a tick are kept for when you tick it again.

## Holidays

**Holidays** says which public holidays count as days off for you: *No public holidays*, *Germany* (the days that count everywhere in Germany) or one of the 16 parts of Germany, such as *Bavaria*. Until you choose, your organization's choice counts; **Reset to default** goes back to it. Under the choice you see the next three holidays that count for you, so the choice is easy to check. Bugaboo works the holidays out itself; it asks no outside service. A holiday of only one town or one region, such as the Augsburg Peace Festival, is not among them: enter it as an away time.

*Holidays* is not part of *Region*, which is only about how dates and numbers are written.

## Away times

An away time is a stretch of days on which you do not work. **Add away time** opens a window with **From** and **To**, and for each end whether it is a whole day: the first day can be **Afternoon only**, the last day **Morning only**. For a single day, type it in **From** and leave **To** empty. A **Note** is possible, and only you see it.

**Bugaboo stores no reason.** It does not know whether you are on holiday, at a course or ill, and others see only *Away* with its days. Nobody else sees your note, also not the people who may enter work times for you.

The page lists the coming away times first, then those of the past year, each with **Change** and **Delete**. Deleting offers **Undo** for a few seconds. An away time is deleted by Bugaboo one year after its last day.

## Shares in projects

A share says how much of your work hours belong to one project, for example 60 % for *Mobile app*. Your project leads set them on **Project settings › Project members**; at the start every project counts in full (100 %). Your page shows the shares that are set; you see them but cannot change them. When they add up to more than 100 %, the page says so, and your project leads see that too, without seeing the names of projects they may not see.

A share counts as nothing while you have no role in its project, and counts again when a role comes back.

## For someone else

A person with the permission **Enter work times for others** (at the start only Administrators) opens the same page from **Organization settings › People**, the page of a person, **Work times of …**. The page says "You are changing the work times of Lea. Lea is told." They can change the usual work days and enter, change and delete away times; Lea gets a notification each time. They never see or write a note. Other people see Lea's work times only as *Who sees people* allows for *Work times*, and only through the projects they share with Lea.

## Project members

**Project settings › Project members** lists every person who has a role in the project, directly or through a group, with their *Share in the project*. A person who may decide who has which role there types a whole number from 0 to 100 into the box; it saves when you leave the box, with **Undo**. A person whose shares add up to more than 100 % is marked "Over 100 % in all", with the other projects you may see, and "projects you can't see" for the others.
