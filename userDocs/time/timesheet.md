# The timesheet

The *Timesheet* shows your week in one grid. Open it from the menu, or press `g` then `t`. *Previous week*, *This week* and *Next week* move through the weeks, which start on your *First day of the week*.

## Work day

The rows *Start*, *End* and *Breaks* are what you type afterwards when you forgot to tap. *Working hours* is worked out for you. Type a time of day like `8:05`, `805` or `5:15 pm`.

*Breaks* shows the total of the day, and you type the times of the breaks: `12:10-12:40`, or several with a comma between them, `10:00-10:15, 12:10-12:40`. A total such as `30m` is refused, because Bugaboo never stores a time you did not give. Typing the breaks again replaces the ones of the day; the old ones stay in its history. A cell saves when you leave it, and says *Saving…* and then *Saved*. A value that cannot be read is marked in the cell with what to type instead, and nothing is saved.

A start for a day that is not today waits for its end, so a past day is saved whole.

## Time on issues

One row for each issue, one column for each day. Type `1:30`, `1.5` or `90m`: all three mean ninety minutes. Clear a cell to take its time back. *Add an issue* finds an issue by its ID or by words of its title. A cell with more than one time entry is shown as the sum and changed in the details of the entries.

The footer compares *Booked on issues* with *Working hours not booked on issues* for each day and for the week.

## Warnings and reasons

When a day breaks a working-time rule, a sentence in the warning color says so under the days. The day is recorded as it happened; a warning never blocks anything.

A change of a past day asks *Why are you changing this day?* before it saves. The old times stay in the history of the day: a day that was changed shows *Changed*, and it opens who changed it, when, the old and new times and why.

## On a phone

The week shows its totals on top and one day at a time below, with a tab for each day.
