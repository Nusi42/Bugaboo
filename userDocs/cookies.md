# Cookies we use

GENERATED by `cargo xtask gen api` from the cookie table in the code (COMP-13). Do not edit this page by hand.

Bugaboo sets only the cookies it needs to work. They are not used to follow you around, to count visits or for advertising, and no other company sets cookies through Bugaboo. That is why Bugaboo shows no cookie banner (PRIV-01, COMP-13).

| Cookie              | What it is for                                                                                          | Who can read it                                                                      | When it is sent                                                                          |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `__Host-bb_session` | Keeps you signed in on this device. Bugaboo removes it when you sign out or when your sign-in runs out. | Only the server.                                                                     | For requests on Bugaboo's own pages, and when you arrive from a link on another website. |
| `__Host-bb_csrf`    | Protects you from other websites that try to get Bugaboo to do something in your name.                  | Only the server and the Bugaboo page itself, which needs it to protect your changes. | Only for requests that start on Bugaboo's own pages.                                     |

The names start with `__Host-`. This makes your browser keep the cookie to this one Bugaboo address and send it only over a secure (HTTPS) connection.
