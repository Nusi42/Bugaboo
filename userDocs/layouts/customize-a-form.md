# Customizing a form

You need the permission "Manage issue types, kinds of relation and layouts" in the project. Open the editor from the new-issue dialog with **Customize this form**, or from *Project settings › Layouts* (for the whole organization: *Organization settings › Layouts*).

## Making a layout of your own

At first a form has not been customized: it shows the usual fields to every role. Choose the form and the issue type, then press **Customize for** the project. Bugaboo makes a copy of the layout that counts there now, so you can change it without touching the place it came from. **Use the inherited layout again** takes your layout away.

## Sections and tabs

A layout is a list of **sections**. Give a section a title, and put it under a **tab** by typing the tab's name: sections with the same tab are shown together under it, and a form without tabs has none. The title and the issue type are always asked for.

## Moving fields

Each field has a handle. Drag it into a section or onto the list *Fields that are not on this form* to take it off. Everything a drag does has buttons too, so it works with a keyboard and on a phone, where there are no handles: **Move up**, **Move down**, **Move to another section**, **Take off this form**, and **Add to a section** for the fields that are not on the form.

## Roles

Under each field is a list of roles with a box each: the roles the field is shown to (on the new-issue form: asked from). A field you add starts with every role ticked.

## Saving

Nothing changes for anybody until you press **Save layout**. While you have the editor open, nobody else can change that layout: they are told who is editing, and can ask you to finish or, with the right permission, edit anyway.
