# Layouts

A **layout** says which fields a form shows, in which sections, and for which roles. There are two forms: the **new-issue form** (what people are asked for when they make an issue) and the **issue page**. A Guest can get a short form and a Team member the full one.

- [Customizing a form](customize-a-form.md): the editor, sections and tabs, moving fields, and the roles of each field
- [Which layout counts](which-layout-counts.md): projects, sub-projects, issue types and the whole organization
- [Field permissions win](field-permissions-win.md): why a layout never shows a field to a role that may not see it
