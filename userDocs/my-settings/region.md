# Region

Open *My settings*, then "Region". The choices here say how Bugaboo writes times and numbers for you. They are kept with you and follow you to every browser.

## Time zone

Choose the time zone the times you see are shown in. "Match my device" uses the time zone of the device you are using. The list has every time zone Bugaboo knows. At the top of the page, a line shows how a date and a time of day look right now with your choices.

A day begins at midnight in your time zone. On the day the clocks change for summer time, that day has 23 or 25 hours.

## Date format

- "Match my device" writes dates as the device does.
- "Day, month, year (31.12.2026)", "Month, day, year (12/31/2026)" or "Year, month, day (2026-12-31)" write every date in that order.

## Time of day

- "24-hour (14:30)" or "12-hour (2:30 PM)" writes every time of day on that clock.
- "Match my device" follows the date format: with the date format "Match my device" you get the clock of your device, and with any other date format you get 24-hour.

Every date and time of day in Bugaboo, in lists, on the page of an issue, in the history, in notifications and in e-mails, is written with these choices.

## Number format

Choose how a big number and a decimal are written: `1,234.5`, `1.234,5` or `1 234,5`. "Match my device" follows the device.

## First day of the week

Choose the day a week starts on in calendars: Monday, Sunday or Saturday. "Match my device" follows the device. A search for "this week" or "last week" starts its week on this day too.

## Reset to default

Every choice has a button **Reset to default** that takes your choice away. Your organization can also set a default for everybody; your own choice always wins, except for a setting an administrator locked (**Set by your organization**).
