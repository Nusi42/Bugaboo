# My data

*My settings › My data* gives you a copy of everything Bugaboo stores about you. The law calls this your right of access (GDPR Art. 15) and your right to take your data elsewhere (Art. 20).

## Download my data

Choose **Download my data**. Bugaboo creates the file in the background; for years of work the file is large, so this can take a while. While it does, the button says **Creating the file** and the rest of the page stays where it is. You can leave the page. When the file is ready, Bugaboo tells you under *Notifications* and by e-mail; the notification has **Open My data**, and the page itself shows **Download the file**, with the size of the file and the day until which it is kept.

You can ask for one file at a time. While one is being created, ask again when it is ready.

## What the file holds

The file is a ZIP file. Open it on your computer:

- `index.html` opens in your browser and leads to one readable page for each kind of record: your profile and settings, your sign-ins and sessions, the issues you reported, the comments and texts you wrote with every version, your time entries, work days and away times, your notifications, saved searches, agents, the entries of the security log that name you, and more.
- The folder `data` holds the same records as JSON files, which another program can read.
- The folder `files` holds the files you uploaded, named by their ID. Their names are in `data/attachment.json`.
- `README.txt` says what each field means, and for each kind of record why it is kept, on which legal basis, how long it is kept and who receives it.

Text written with the assistant keeps its mark in the file. Keys and secrets are never in it: a passkey is listed without its key, an access key without its secret, and your password is not there at all.

### Other people in the file

Other people appear only as you may see them in Bugaboo today: by name, as *Someone (their role)*, or as *Someone*. If you see people of one kind only by their role, or not at all, the file names nobody. Texts other people wrote are not copied; the file only says where they mention you.

## Download the file

The download button gives you an address that works for a few minutes and only for you. The file itself is kept for **7 days** after you asked for it, then it is deleted.

## When an administrator downloads your data

An administrator with the permission *Download someone's data* can create the same file for you, for example when you asked for it by letter. They must always give a reason. You are told in the app and by e-mail, with the reason, and the security log records who did it and why.

## What is kept and for how long

Under the download, *My data* shows every kind of data Bugaboo keeps for a time, in two tables: what Bugaboo deletes by itself after its period, and what an administrator deletes after its period (working hours and time on issues). Each row says how long the kind is kept, what happens then and why. The periods are those of your organization; an administrator sets them on [Keep and delete rules](../privacy/keep-rules-page.md).

## Where to correct it

The last part of the page says where you correct each kind of your data: your name and e-mail address on [Profile](profile.md), your usual work days and away times on *Work times*, your working hours and time on issues on the timesheet (a past day with a reason), and your choices under *My settings*. Anything else an administrator corrects for you, with a reason; you are told.
