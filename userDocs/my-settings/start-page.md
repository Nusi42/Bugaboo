# Start page

Open *My settings*, then "Start page". Choose the page that opens after you sign in. The list holds the pages of Bugaboo you can open, the same pages quick search finds.

Until you choose, Bugaboo opens Home. If you can no longer open the page you chose, you see a page that says it was not found, and the page offers a way back to Home. **Reset to default** brings Home back.

Your start page is kept with you, so it is the same on every device.
