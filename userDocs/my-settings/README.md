# My settings

*My settings* holds the choices that are only yours. Bugaboo keeps them with you, on the server, so they follow you to every browser and every device you sign in from, and nobody who uses the same computer after you sees them.

Open *My settings* from the menu. It has these pages:

- [Appearance](../basics/appearance.md): themes, spacing, larger text, less motion and where an issue opens
- [Region](region.md): time zone, how dates and numbers are written, the first day of the week
- [Start page](start-page.md): the page that opens after you sign in
- [Keyboard shortcuts](../basics/keyboard-shortcuts.md): see and change the keys
- [Sign-in and security](../sign-in/sign-in.md): password, passkeys, two-step sign-in, where you're signed in

## Reset to default

Every choice that is yours has a button **Reset to default**. It takes away your choice, so the value from above counts again: the one your organization chose, or Bugaboo's standard value. Under each setting a line says where the value you see comes from: *Bugaboo's standard value*, *Your organization's choice* or *Your own choice*.

## Set by your organization

An administrator can lock a setting for everyone, for example the theme. You then see the value, but you cannot change it, and the page says so with **Set by your organization**. Ask an administrator if you need it changed.

## Signing out

When you sign out, your choices are removed from the browser. The pages before sign-in use the look of the device.
