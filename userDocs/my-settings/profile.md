# Profile

*My settings › Profile* shows your name and your e-mail address, and lets you correct them. The law calls this your right to have your data corrected (GDPR Art. 16).

## Your name

Change the name and leave the box: the new name counts at once, everywhere you appear. If you changed it by mistake, choose **Undo**. The history keeps the change, and so does the security log.

Who sees your name does not change: people who see names see the new one, and people who see only a role or *Someone* still see that.

## Your e-mail address

You sign in with your e-mail address, and e-mails from Bugaboo go to it. To change it:

1. Choose **Change e-mail address**, type the new address and your password, and choose **Send link**.
2. Open the e-mail that goes to the new address and the link in it. The link works once, for one hour.
3. From then on you sign in with the new address. Until then, the old address keeps working.

When the change is done, the old address gets an e-mail that says so, with the time. If that was not you, open your sessions in *My settings* and choose **Sign out everywhere**, then ask an administrator for help. Changing the address does not sign you out anywhere.

If another account already has the new address, the answer on the page is the same, and the e-mail to that address says that an account exists there. That way nobody can find out which addresses have an account. Asking for a new address replaces a link you asked for before, so only the newest link works. An address gets at most one such e-mail in a while (see [Times and counts of notifications](../notifications/numbers.md)); if you asked twice in a row, use the link of the first e-mail.

While e-mail is switched off in your organization, no link can be sent, so you cannot change the address yourself. The page says so: ask an administrator, who can change it for you.

## Corrections by an administrator

An administrator with the permission *Manage people* can correct the name and the e-mail address of others on the page of the person, always with a reason. Administrators change their own name and address here, like everybody else. A corrected address counts at once, and both the old and the new address get an e-mail that says so. You are told in the app and by e-mail with the reason (*An administrator corrected your name or e-mail address*); you cannot switch this off. The security log records who corrected what, and keeps only a fingerprint of the old value, never the value itself.

## Other data about you

Work days are corrected with a reason (see [Working hours](../working-hours/README.md)), work times and away times on their own page, and your choices in the other pages of *My settings*. To see everything Bugaboo stores about you, see [My data](my-data.md).
