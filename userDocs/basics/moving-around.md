# Finding your way around

Bugaboo looks a little different on a computer, a tablet and a phone, but it has the same places and the same words everywhere. Only places that exist are shown: you never see a menu entry that leads nowhere.

## On a computer

A **menu** stands on the left. It has a search button, the start page, *My settings* and, for administrators, *Organization settings*. Press "Narrow the menu" to keep only the icons and give the page more room; press "Widen the menu" to bring the words back. Bugaboo remembers your choice on this device.

The bar at the top says where you are, for example "My settings › Appearance". Every part except the last one can be pressed to take you one step up.

## On a tablet

The menu is hidden to give the page the whole width. Press "Menu" at the top to slide it in. Press "Esc", or pick a place, and it closes again. "Search" is a button in the top bar.

## On a phone

Bugaboo shows one page at a time. A bar with labelled buttons sits at the bottom: "Home", "Search" and "More". "More" opens a sheet with *My settings*, *Organization settings* (for administrators), "Keyboard shortcuts" and "Sign out".

On a page that is part of another one, "Back" at the top takes you one step back. The back gesture of your phone does the same.

## Quick search

Press `Ctrl+K` (or the search button) to open quick search. It finds issues, saved searches, projects, settings pages and a few actions, in groups with their kind above them:

- Type the ID of an issue, such as "WEB-142", to open it.
- Type words of a title to see the first ten issues whose title has them. The list follows you when you stop typing for a moment.
- The actions are *Create issue* and *Sign out*. The pages include *Notification settings*.

Use the arrow keys to pick a result and press Enter. "Esc" closes quick search and puts you back where you were. Quick search shows only what you may see: an issue you may not see is not found, exactly like one that does not exist.

## Settings

*My settings* is for you: how Bugaboo looks, your keyboard shortcuts, your sign-in and security, and the places where you're signed in. *Organization settings* is for the whole Bugaboo server and is only there for administrators. Both have a menu by topic, in plain words. On a phone the topics are a page of their own and each topic opens on its own page.

## Opening one item next to a list

Some pages show a list and open a simple item in a **side panel** next to it, so the list stays in view. Press "Esc" or the close button to close the panel; the focus returns to the item you opened. On a phone the panel takes the whole page.

Big items open in the **focus view**: the item gets most of the page, the menu shrinks to icons and a slim list stays on the left. "Hide list" gives even more room, "Back to list" (or "Esc") returns to the list, and "Previous" and "Next" move through it.

## Undo

Things that can be taken back happen at once and show an "Undo" button for 10 seconds. Bugaboo only asks "Are you sure?" for things that cannot be undone.
