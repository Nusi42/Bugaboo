# Finding your way around

Bugaboo looks a little different on a computer, a tablet and a phone, but it has the same places and the same words everywhere. Only places that exist are shown: you never see a menu entry that leads nowhere.

## On a computer

A **menu** stands on the left. It has a search button, the start page, *My settings* and, for administrators, *Organization settings*. Press "Narrow the menu" to keep only the icons and give the page more room; press "Widen the menu" to bring the words back. Bugaboo remembers your choice on this device.

The bar at the top says where you are, for example "My settings › Appearance". Every part except the last one is a link that takes you one step up.

## On a tablet

The menu is hidden to give the page the whole width. Press "Menu" at the top to slide it in. Press "Esc", or pick a place, and it closes again. "Search" is a button in the top bar.

## On a phone

Bugaboo shows one screen at a time. A bar with labelled buttons sits at the bottom: "Home", "Search" and "More". "More" opens a sheet with *My settings*, *Organization settings* (for administrators), "Keyboard shortcuts" and "Sign out".

On a screen that is part of another one, "Back" at the top takes you one step back. The back gesture of your phone does the same.

## Quick search

Press `Ctrl+K` (or `/`, or the search button) to open quick search. Type a few letters of a page, such as "appearance", use the arrow keys to pick one and press Enter. "Esc" closes quick search and puts you back where you were. Quick search shows only pages you may open.

## Settings

*My settings* is for you: how Bugaboo looks, your keyboard shortcuts, your sign-in and security, and the places where you are signed in. *Organization settings* is for the whole Bugaboo server and is only there for administrators. Both have a menu by topic, in plain words. On a phone the topics are a page of their own and each topic opens on its own screen.

## Opening one item next to a list

Some screens show a list and open a simple item in a **side panel** next to it, so the list stays in view. Press "Esc" or the close button to close the panel; the focus returns to the item you opened. On a phone the panel takes the whole screen.

Big items open in the **focus view**: the item gets most of the screen, the menu shrinks to icons and a slim list stays on the left. "Hide list" gives even more room, "Back to list" (or "Esc") returns to the list, and "Previous" and "Next" move through it.

## Undo

Things that can be taken back happen at once and show an "Undo" button for 10 seconds. Bugaboo only asks "Are you sure?" for things that cannot be undone.
