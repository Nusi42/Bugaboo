# Appearance

Open *My settings*, then "Appearance".

## Themes

Bugaboo has four themes: **Daylight** and **Paper** are light, **Dusk** and **Midnight** are dark. Every theme is checked so that text stays readable.

Choose **Match my device** (the usual choice): Bugaboo then uses a light theme when your device is set to light and a dark theme when it is set to dark, and follows the device when that changes. Or choose **Always light** or **Always dark** to use a theme whatever the device says. Under "Theme when my device is light" and "Theme when my device is dark" you pick the theme for each case from small samples that show how each theme looks.

The pages you see before signing in have the choice of theme at the bottom, so you can pick a theme before you sign in.

## Own themes

Besides the four themes, your organization may offer **own themes**, and you can create **personal** ones. An own theme starts from one of the four and changes only some of its colors, its shadow, its corners or its choice among the built-in fonts; everything else stays as in the theme it starts from, so later improvements of that theme reach it too.

- A theme **for everyone** is created by an administrator in *Organization settings › Look › Themes*. Everybody in the organization can choose it.
- A **personal** theme is created in *My settings › Appearance*. Only you see it. You can keep up to 10.

Own themes are offered with the four themes: a light own theme under "Theme when my device is light", a dark one under "Theme when my device is dark".

Every pair of colors is rated for **readability**: *Easy*, *OK* or *Hard to read*. A theme for everyone can't be saved while a pair is *Hard to read*. A personal theme can, and Bugaboo names the pairs so you can mend them.

**Save a copy** keeps a theme as a new one of your own to change. **Download** saves a theme as a file, and **Upload** creates a theme from such a file (at most 64 KB); a value a theme may not have is named and the file is not taken.

If a theme you chose is deleted, Bugaboo shows the theme it started from instead.

## Spacing

"Comfortable" is the usual spacing. "Compact" fits more on the page. Every page follows your choice, the lists of issues and saved searches included.

## Larger text

Switch on "Larger text" to enlarge all text. Buttons and spacing keep their shape.

## Less motion

Switch on "Less motion" to turn off the short movements, such as a panel sliding in. If your device already asks for less motion, Bugaboo follows it without any switch.

## More contrast

If your device asks for more contrast, Bugaboo gives text and lines in every theme a stronger color. There is nothing to switch on in Bugaboo.

## Where an issue opens

Choose whether an issue opens in the **Focus view**, which gives it most of the page, or in a **Side panel** next to the list.

## Where your choices are kept

Your choices are kept with you, on the server. They follow you to every browser and device you sign in from, and when you sign out they are removed from the browser. Every choice has a button **Reset to default**. An administrator can lock a setting for everyone; you then see **Set by your organization** and cannot change it. See [My settings](../my-settings/README.md).
