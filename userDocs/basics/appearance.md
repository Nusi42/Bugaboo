# Appearance

Open *My settings*, then "Appearance".

## Themes

Bugaboo has four themes: **Daylight** and **Paper** are light, **Dusk** and **Midnight** are dark. Every theme is checked so that text stays readable.

Choose one, or choose **Match my device**: Bugaboo then uses a light theme when your device is set to light and a dark theme when it is set to dark, and follows the device when that changes. Under "Theme when my device is light" and "Theme when my device is dark" you choose which theme each case uses.

The pages you see before signing in have the choice of theme at the bottom, so you can pick a theme before you sign in.

## Spacing

"Comfortable" is the usual spacing. "Compact" fits more on the screen.

## Larger text

Switch on "Larger text" to make all text bigger. Buttons and spacing keep their shape.

## Less motion

Switch on "Less motion" to turn off the short movements, such as a panel sliding in. If your device already asks for less motion, Bugaboo follows it without any switch.

## More contrast

If your device asks for more contrast, Bugaboo gives text and lines in every theme a stronger color. There is nothing to switch on in Bugaboo.

## Where your choices are kept

For now your choices are kept in this browser, on this device. They do not follow you to another device yet.
