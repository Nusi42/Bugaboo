# Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcuts are extras: everything they do also has a button on the page.

## Seeing all shortcuts

Press `?` to open the list of all keyboard shortcuts, or press "Keyboard shortcuts" in the menu. The list says what each shortcut does and where it works. It is always up to date, because it is the same list Bugaboo itself uses.

## Sequences

Some shortcuts are two keys in a row. Press the first key, let go, and press the second within a second. For example `g` and then `h` goes to the start page.

## While you type

A shortcut does nothing while a text box has the focus, so typing never starts something by accident.

## Choosing your own keys

Open *My settings*, then "Keyboard shortcuts". The list can be searched. Open a shortcut to see its keys now and its usual keys. Click the box "New keys" and press the keys you want: one key, or two in a row. Bugaboo tells you when another shortcut already uses those keys, and does not let you take them. Press "Use these keys" to keep them. The old keys then stop working. "Use the usual keys" brings them back.

Your own keys are kept on this device for now.
