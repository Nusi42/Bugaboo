# Projects

A **project** is a place where work happens. Projects can hold **sub-projects**, as deep as you like, and what people may do follows the projects: a role given in a project counts in the projects inside it, unless the person who gave it said otherwise.

- [Making projects and sub-projects](making-projects.md): names, short names and where they sit
- [Moving a project](moving-a-project.md): what you read before it happens, and when a move is not allowed

## What you see

You see exactly the projects you may see. In the menu on the left (on a phone, under "More") they stand as a tree: press the arrow in front of a project to open the projects inside it. Bugaboo remembers what you opened on this device. The tree never says how many projects are inside a project, because that number could include projects you may not see.

A project that is inside a project you may not see stands at the top of your tree, and its address and "where you are" line never name the project above it.

If you open the address of a project you may not see, you get "Not found", exactly as for an address that does not exist.

## The pages of a project

- **Overview** (`/p/WEB`): the name, the short name, where the project sits and its sub-projects. A project without sub-projects offers one next step: "Make a sub-project", for people who may.
- **Project settings** (`/p/WEB/settings`): the name, the short name and "Move", for the people who may change them, normally the project leads.
- **Organization settings › Projects**: the list of the projects you see, with search, for administrators. Open one to change its name; make a project at the top level below the list.
