# Making projects and sub-projects

## At the top level

*Organization settings › Projects* has the form "Make a project" below the list. It needs the permission "Make projects at the top level", which only administrators have at the start. Type a **name** and a **short name** and press "Make the project".

## Inside another project

Open the project and press "Make a sub-project". It needs the permission "Make sub-projects inside the project" in that project. Project leads have it in their projects and in the projects inside them.

## The short name

The short name starts the issue IDs of the project (`WEB-142`) and is its address (`/p/WEB`).

- It has 2 to 10 capital letters or digits and starts with a letter: `WEB`, `APP2`.
- You may type small letters; the form turns them into capitals.
- It is unique in the whole organization, also among the old short names of other projects.
- It can be changed later in *Project settings*. **The old short name stays with the project**: its old addresses keep working (they open the project and the address bar shows the new one), and nobody else can take it. The project itself can take it back.

Changing a short name needs the permission "Change the project's name, short name and settings" and the right to make projects where the project sits. Only people who may make projects there are told that a short name is taken, and they are told the same thing whether the project that has it is one they can see or not.

## The name

The name can be changed at any time. If two people change a project at the same time, the second one is told that someone changed it first and can look again.
