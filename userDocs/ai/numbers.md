The limits of the assistant and of agents, as Bugaboo works by them. An administrator can set a lower maximum for what an agent may do (*Organization settings › Connections › Agents*).

## The assistant

| What                                   | Number                                                      |
| -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| A question to the assistant is at most | 4000 characters                                             |
| A conversation keeps at most           | 20 turns                                                    |
| A turn is at most                      | 8000 characters                                             |
| The assistant looks things up at most  | 8 times for one question                                    |
| The assistant proposes at most         | 10 changes at once; a proposal waits 10 minutes for *Apply* |
| The calls of tools are kept for        | 90 days                                                     |
