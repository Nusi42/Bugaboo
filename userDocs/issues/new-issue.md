# Creating a new issue

Press "New issue" in the menu (or in the bar at the bottom of a phone), or press `c` anywhere. The form opens as a **window** over the page, and as a whole page with "Back" on a phone.

1. **Project.** The project you looked at last is chosen. "Change" lets you search for another.
2. **Type.** The first issue type is chosen; pick another one from the list.
3. **Fields.** The title says "(required)" in words. You can also write a description and choose a priority. In a window that is 880 wide or more, the title and the description stand at the left and the other fields at the side.

"Create" creates the issue and opens it the way you chose in *My settings* ("Open issues in"). "Create and start another" creates it and leaves the dialog open with the same project and type, so you can write the next one.

## The window

At the top right of the window are "Window size", "Minimize", "Maximize" and "Close". "Minimize" parks the window as a bar at the bottom of the page, with its title and "Draft kept"; press the bar and the window is back with everything you typed. "Maximize" lets it fill the page; "Restore down" gives it its size back. Drag a side or a corner to change the size, or press "Window size" and choose "Wider", "Narrower", "Taller" or "Shorter" (the keys Ctrl and Alt with an arrow key do the same). Bugaboo remembers the size you gave the window, for you, on every device. The buttons stay at the bottom while the form scrolls; the main button, "Create", is the last one.

## Your draft

Whatever you write is kept as a draft while you write: in your browser and, privately, on the server. "Your draft is kept if you close this window." The next time you open the dialog for that project it says "Continue your draft from yesterday", and you can continue or start again. The draft is gone as soon as you press "Create", and in any case 30 days after its last change.

If your role may not set a field you chose, Bugaboo says so when you press "Create" and keeps what you wrote.
