# Comments, mentions, history and following

## Comments

You can write a comment on an issue you may see if your role has "Comment". A comment uses the same writing as the description: simple formatting, web addresses and issue IDs such as `WEB-12`. An issue ID shows as a chip only when you may see that issue; for everyone else it stays plain text.

- You can change and delete **your own** comments. While you edit, the comment is locked for you, like a description.
- Every save keeps the earlier text. Open **Earlier versions** on a comment to read them.
- A deleted comment goes to the trash and **Undo** brings it back.

## Mentions

Type `@` in a comment to name someone. Bugaboo offers people, groups and roles you may name; choose one and the whole name is written for you.

- Naming a **group** or a **role** tells everyone in it.
- What you may name follows *Who sees people*. If people may not see each other's names, for example as a guest, you can name a role but not a person. A name that cannot be resolved stays plain text.
- Comments that name you are listed under **Mentions** on the start page, the unread ones first. **Mark as read** clears the *New* marks.

Nothing is sent by e-mail yet, and there is no bell: that comes with the next version.

## History

An issue's **History** has three tabs: **Everything**, **Comments** and **Changes**. It is newest first and shows who changed which field, from what to what, and the relations and sub-issues added or taken away.

You see only what you may see: a change to a field your role cannot see is left out, and so is a person whose name is hidden from you (it reads *someone*).

## Following

You **follow** an issue to keep track of it. You follow the issues you report, the issues assigned to you and the issues you comment on, and you can follow or stop following any issue you may see with the **Follow** button. The issue shows who follows it, as far as you may see people.
