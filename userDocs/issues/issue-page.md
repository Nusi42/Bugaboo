# The issue page and the list of issues

## The list of a project

Open a project and press "Issues of this project". Above the list stand the search bar and the count line, for example "302 in Website · 13 shown": how many issues the search finds, among the issues you may read, and how many of them are on the page now. Under it the list is a grid with one line for each issue: a box to tick, the status icon, the issue ID, the title, the priority, the people it is assigned to and when it changed. The type and the labels are not in the list at first, so that the title has room; "Customize" adds them, and any other field you may read. A line is tinted by its status group and has a colored strip at the left for a high or critical priority; the Legend beside the "New issue" button says what the colors mean, and the icons say the same in words for people who use a reader. A whole line opens its issue. More issues are read as you scroll, and only the lines you can see are drawn, so a list of tens of thousands stays fast. With the arrow keys, or `j` and `k`, you move from line to line, and Enter opens the issue. The list is one stop for the Tab key.

On a computer you shape the list. Press the name of a column to sort by it; the search bar shows the order as `sort by`, and a second press turns it around. Drag the right side of a column name to widen or narrow the column, or press the left and right arrow keys on the handle that stands there. "Customize" opens the panel of columns, beside the list where the window has room for both and under it where it has not: tick the fields you want, move them up and down with the buttons, and press "Back to the project's columns" to undo your changes. The ID and the title stay. "Group by" sorts the lines into groups by any field you may read, each with its number; the groups "In progress", "Not started" and "Done" (in that order) are the usual ones, and priorities come with the highest first. A group folds with a press on its header, or with Enter on it, and a folded group is left out of the lines shown and ticked. "Spacing" is "Comfortable" or "Compact". The columns, widths, spacing and grouping are in the address, so a list that you share shows it as you see it, and "Save search" keeps them with the search, and "Save" on a saved search you changed keeps the new columns and grouping with its text. "Remove all filters" on the list of a project that has a search of its own shows every issue, not the project's search again. The box in the head of the list ticks the lines shown; then "Tick all 302 results" ticks every issue the search finds, up to 5,000. On a phone each issue is a card of two lines, in the same order; columns are not offered, but "Group by" and "Spacing" are. Ticked issues are kept until you clear them.

The "Search" page in the menu is the same list over every project you may see, with a "Project" column.

## Opening an issue

An issue opens in the **focus view** (it takes most of the page, the menu shrinks to icons and a slim list of the project stays on the left; `j` and `k` move through that list, "Back to list" or Esc returns) or in the **side panel** (it opens next to the list, so the list stays in view). Choose which one you like in *My settings › Appearance*, under "Open issues in". "Open in the side panel" and "Open full page" in the header switch from one to the other. The address of the focus view, for example `/issues/WEB-142`, is the one to share: the issue ID in the header copies it (the button is called *WEB-142, copy the address* and says *Address copied*).

On a phone an issue always takes the whole page, and a bar at the bottom offers "Status", "Comment" and "More".

## Changing a value where it stands

Click the title to change it. The big button under the title shows the status with its icon and color; press it to see the changes you can apply and pick one. The **Details** are rows with the name at the left and the value at the right: pick another priority from its list, and choose who the issue is assigned to with "Change" (you can pick only people of the project). Each change shows at once and says "Saving…" and then "Saved · 10:42" where you changed it. For the priority and the people, "Undo" stands in the same row for ten seconds; for the status and the title it is in the message at the bottom. On a phone the Details are closed behind "All details", which still says who the issue is assigned to and its priority.

A field you can see and may not change shows a lock and says so. A field you may not see is left out. Where Bugaboo may not tell you who somebody is, you see their role ("a Team member") or "Someone".

If somebody changed the same value a moment before you, nothing of theirs is overwritten: the page says, for example, "Ben changed Priority to Critical 2 minutes ago." and gives you two buttons, "Keep Ben's" and "Use mine".

## Writing the description

Press "Edit the description" (or `e`) to open the description for writing. Nothing is locked until you type the first letter, so just looking never stops anybody. While you write:

- Your text is kept as a **draft**, in your browser and, privately, on the server, until you save or throw it away (at most 30 days). If you close the window and come back, "Continue your draft from yesterday" is offered.
- If somebody else is editing the same description, you see "Ana is editing the description · since 2 min" and the buttons "Ask Ana to finish" and, if your role allows it, "Edit anyway". Ana is told within 15 seconds; with "Edit anyway" her unsaved text stays her draft.
- If your editing time runs out, somebody took over, or the description changed after you started, the page says "Not saved yet", keeps your text and offers "Compare and save": your text next to the newer one, paragraph by paragraph, with the words that differ marked (underlined and filled, so color is not the only sign), to choose what to keep.
- Every 30 seconds, and when you come back to the tab, Bugaboo checks whether the issue changed. If the description did, you see "Ben changed the description after you started" (the name is shown the way you may see people) with "Show changes", which marks the words that differ.

"Cancel" throws your text away.
