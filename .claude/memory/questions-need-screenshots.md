When the lead asks the maintainer a question about something on screen (a review page question, a follow-up for their look), it shows screenshots of the problem: the bad case, and where useful the alternatives side by side.

**Why:** the maintainer (2026-11-16, on the M8 review page) could not answer F227, F229 and the scroll-bar strip from words alone: "when u ask me questions like these, I will need screenshots showcasing the problem."

**How to apply:** before putting a UX question on a review page or in chat, have a builder or the screenshot specs take the pictures (light and dark, computer and phone where it matters) and put them next to the question. Related: [[user-reviews-on-phone]], [[ux-quality-gate]].
