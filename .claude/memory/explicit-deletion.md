The maintainer's view (2026-10-31, M6 working hours): as long as data is relevant for the company, keeping it is a legitimate right under the GDPR, and only the company can judge relevance. So Bugaboo never deletes such data by itself: a keep period is the minimum, and after it an administrator deletes explicitly (Bugaboo shows what is past the period and makes deleting one step, logged). A removed person's work days keep their name for inspections (ArbZG §17).

**Why:** storage limitation is the organization's judgement, not the software's.

**How to apply:** when a feature proposes an automatic delete job for business records, propose explicit deletion with a count of what is past its period instead, and ask. See [[germany-gdpr-worktime]].
